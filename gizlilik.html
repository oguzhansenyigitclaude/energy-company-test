3. Verilerin nerede saklanıyor

Verilerin Google Firebase Realtime Database üzerinde, Avrupa Birliği (europe-west1) sunucularında saklanır. Bağlantılar şifrelidir (HTTPS/TLS). Güvenlik kuralları başka bir oyuncunun senin kaydını okumasına veya yazmasına izin vermez; yalnız herkese açık karne bilgileri (şirket adı, şirket değeri, tesis sayısı) görünür. Kasan, ciron ve stokların gizlidir.

Verilerin, sen silene kadar saklanır.

🗑️ Oyun içinden silme: Menü → Hesabı Sıfırla → onayla. Bu işlem oyun kaydını, tesislerini ve oyuncu kaydını hem cihazdan hem sunucudan kalıcı olarak siler ve geri alınamaz.

✉️ E-posta ile silme talebi: CompanyGamesLab@gmail.com adresine şirket adını yazarak talep gönderebilirsin. Talepler 30 gün içinde işlenir.

Not: Sohbete yazdığın mesajlar ve başka oyunculara gönderdiğin postalar, diğer oyuncuların kutularında kalabilir — tıpkı gönderilmiş bir mesaj gibi. Silme talebinde bunların da kaldırılmasını isteyebilirsin.

⚡ Energy Company — Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 August 2026 · Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Who we are

Energy Company is an energy company management simulation developed by Company Games Studio. Contact: CompanyGamesLab@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Required to play

  • An anonymous account ID (Firebase Anonymous) — not linked to your real identity
  • The company name you choose, your in-game progress (money, plants, licences, research)
  • A last-seen timestamp, so other players see whether you are online
  • The country and province you chose inside the game map — this is not your device's real location

Only if you use them

  • Google Sign-In (optional): your email address, name and Google account ID, via Google Firebase Authentication, used solely to restore your save on another device. This is never shown to other players. The full game is playable without signing in.
  • Chat messages and in-game mail (visible to other players), friendships, alliance membership
  • Reports (🚩) and suggestions (💡) you choose to send us

What we never collect

Your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, browsing history or other apps on your device. The game contains no ad networks, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. Your data is never used for advertising and is never sold.

3. Where your data is stored

On Google Firebase Realtime Database, in the European Union (europe-west1). All connections use HTTPS/TLS. Security rules prevent any other player from reading or writing your save; only public profile facts (company name, company value, facility counts) are visible. Your cash, revenue and stock levels are private.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — accounts and game saves
  • Google Identity Services — optional Google Sign-In
  • Open-Meteo — real weather forecasts. Only the latitude/longitude of a map tile is sent. No personal data and not your device location.
  • FormSubmit — delivers the suggestion form to us by email, only if you send one

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete it.

  • In-app: Menu → Reset Account → confirm. This permanently deletes your save, facilities and player record from both the device and the server. It cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to CompanyGamesLab@gmail.com with your company name. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Note: messages you posted in chat or sent to other players may remain in their inboxes, like any sent message. You can ask us to remove those as part of a deletion request.

6. Interacting with other players

The game has chat, mail and alliances. If someone behaves badly you can 🚩 Report them or 🚫 Block them from their company card. Blocking hides their chat messages and mail from you and can be undone at any time.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided data, we delete the record.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (KVKK in Türkiye, GDPR in Europe) you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Contact us at CompanyGamesLab@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes. We announce significant changes through in-game mail.
